How to use Extract PDF Pages

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Select the pages to keep by clicking the previews or typing a range.
  3. Click “Extract pages” and download the new PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is the page order kept?

Yes, extracted pages keep their original order.

What is the difference from Split PDF?

Extract creates one new PDF from your selection. Split creates several files.
