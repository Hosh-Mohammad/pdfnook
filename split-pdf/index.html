How to use Split PDF

  1. Upload the PDF you want to split.
  2. Choose a split mode: ranges, every page, or every N pages.
  3. Click “Split PDF” and download the result (multiple files arrive as a ZIP).

Frequently asked questions

How do I write page ranges?

Use commas and dashes, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10. Each range becomes its own PDF.

What do I get when I split into many files?

A single ZIP archive containing one PDF per range or chunk.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, without recompression.
