Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

This policy explains what information pdfnook (“we”, “the site”) handles when you use the website.

1. Your files

All PDF and image processing is performed locally in your web browser. Your files are not uploaded to our servers or to any third party. Files exist only in your device’s memory while you use a tool and are discarded when you reset the tool, navigate away or close the page. We do not store, view or share your documents.

2. Information we collect

We do not require registration and do not ask for personal information to use the tools. The site may receive standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) through our hosting provider’s server logs, which is used to operate and secure the service.

3. Local storage

The site stores one small preference in your browser’s local storage: your choice of light or dark theme. It never contains personal data or file contents and stays on your device.

4. Advertising and cookies

We may show ads served by Google AdSense in the future. If ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com. You may also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent through a consent banner before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used. Until advertising is enabled, this site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Third-party links

The site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

6. Children

The site is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data through the tools, there is generally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond as required by applicable law.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact page.
