About pdfnook

pdfnook is a collection of free online PDF tools. You can merge, split, compress, convert, rotate, edit and protect PDF files without creating an account or installing software.

Our approach

Most online PDF services upload your documents to a server. pdfnook does the opposite: every tool runs inside your own browser using open-source libraries (PDF.js, pdf-lib and JSZip). Your files never leave your device.

What we promise

  • Free to use, with no sign-up and no watermarks.
  • Honest descriptions: every tool states what it can and cannot do.
  • No selling or sharing of your documents, because we never receive them.

Funding

To cover running costs, pdfnook may display advertising in the future. Ads will always be clearly labelled and kept separate from the tools.
